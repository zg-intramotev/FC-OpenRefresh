# Flat icons: source and licence

The SVG icons in this folder come from **FreeCAD-Flat-Icons**:
<https://github.com/obelisk79/FreeCAD-Flat-Icons>

They are dedicated to the public domain under the **Creative Commons CC0 1.0
Universal** dedication; the full text is in `LICENSE` in this folder. CC0 does
not require attribution, so this credit is a courtesy to the author rather than
a licence condition.

The icons are included **unmodified**. OpenRefresh only selects which of them to
show (it covers about 380 icons for Part, Part Design, Sketcher and the view
commands). They are drawn for dark themes and may look low-contrast on light
themes.

The icon colours follow the open-color palette: <https://github.com/yeun/open-color>
